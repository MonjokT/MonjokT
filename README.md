### What I Bring to the Table

I design and analyze physical infrastructure — **and I use data + AI to make it smarter, more sustainable, and more resilient.**

Most civil engineers don't code. Most data analysts don't understand structural loads, dam hydrology, or material fatigue. I bring both — and that hybrid lens is exactly what modern infrastructure teams need.

**What that looks like in practice:**
- Designing structural systems with **ProtaStructure**, then building **Power BI dashboards** to track project performance metrics
- Conducting **ESG research** on construction lifecycle emissions and translating findings into actionable sustainability strategies
- Performing **dam inspection and maintenance analysis**, using data to predict structural risks before they become failures
- Using **SQL and Excel** to turn raw engineering data into decisions that save time, cost, and carbon
